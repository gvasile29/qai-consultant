# Channel Playbooks

One text is never pasted across channels. Each gets its own version. All rules inherit the global voice (professional-technical, no em dash, disciplined emoji, problem-first).

## LinkedIn (primary channel)

**Audience skew:** test managers, decision makers, and the dev/MCP community all live here.

**Structure:**
1. **Hook line** - a problem/pain, standalone, 1-2 short lines. This is the scroll-stopper.
2. **Context** - name the gap, why today's status quo fails. 2-4 lines.
3. **The turn** - introduce QAI / the new capability as the resolution.
4. **Body** - 2-4 concrete capabilities, each with an emoji section marker and a specific mechanism (not a vague benefit). Use the 🔍 📊 🧠 style markers seen in existing posts.
5. **Proof / differentiator** - deterministic, grounded, keyless, transparent, whichever fits.
6. **CTA** - live app link and/or MCP install line.
7. **Engagement question** - QA-native, pointed at the reader's own work, with a 👇.
8. **Hashtags** - 5-8, end of post. Core set: #QualityAssurance #SoftwareTesting #TestAutomation #QAEngineering #AI plus topic ones (#MCP #ISTQB #EUAIAct #OpenSource #DevOps).

**Length:** medium-long (roughly 150-300 words). Whitespace between blocks, LinkedIn rewards scannability.

**Tone:** the "building in public" voice works well here for release/milestone posts (first person, "the design principle I'm most proud of").

## Facebook

**Audience skew:** Romanian audience, non-specialist decision makers, broader reach.

**Structure:**
- Warmer, plain-language, less jargon than LinkedIn. Explain the value as if to a smart non-QA-specialist.
- Shorter paragraphs, friendly hook.
- Checkmark lists (✅) for what you get.
- Clear single CTA with the live link.
- Fewer hashtags (4-7), simpler (#Testare #QA #AI #Software #MCP #Claude).

**Provide two variants** when useful (matches existing files):
- **Varianta principala** - full version.
- **Varianta scurta** - 2-3 lines for low-attention feeds.

**Language:** Facebook posts are often Romanian-first for the local audience. Offer EN if requested.

## Instagram

**Audience skew:** visual discovery, community, top-of-funnel awareness.

**Structure:**
- **Visual-first.** The carousel or reel carries the message; the caption supports it. Always specify the visual concept (carousel slides or reel beats).
- **Caption:** short and punchy. Lead with the hook line, 1-3 tight sentences of value, one CTA ("link in bio" - the app URL can't be clickable inline).
- **Hashtags:** heavier (10-20), mix broad (#softwaretesting #qa #techtips) and niche (#istqb #mcp #testautomation). Can go in a comment or caption end.
- **Carousel idea pattern:** Slide 1 = the pain (hook). Slides 2-4 = the gap + how QAI closes it, one idea per slide, big text, brand colors. Final slide = CTA + logo.
- **Reel idea pattern:** hook in first 2 seconds (on-screen text of the pain), quick screen capture of the app producing an artifact, payoff line, CTA. Keep under ~30s. See existing `reel_v3.0_script.md` / `facebook_reel_qai_consultant.md` for tone.

**Tone:** slightly warmer and more energetic than LinkedIn, still credible, never cringe.

## Cross-channel rules

- Same core idea, three genuinely different executions.
- Hook can be shared across channels but the body/length/format must be re-shaped per channel.
- Every channel version still passes the SKILL.md quality checklist.
- When Gabi asks for "all channels" on one topic, produce LinkedIn + Facebook + Instagram, each in its own clearly labeled section (or its own file per the naming rule).
